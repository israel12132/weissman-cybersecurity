Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-06-03 · Version 1.0 · GDPR & CCPA aligned

1. Who we are

Weissman Cybersecurity Ltd. ("we", "us") — a security platform for SOC teams. Registered in Israel. Data Protection Officer: dpo@weissman.io.

2. What we collect

Account data

Email, hashed password (bcrypt), MFA seed (encrypted), tenant slug, role, IP at login, last seen.

Scan data

Your-provided Targets (domains, IPs, repo URLs), scan results, findings (descriptions, evidence, proof-of-concept, severity, MITRE technique), endpoint-agent telemetry (process names, network connections, log integrity). All scan data is tenant-scoped and isolated by Postgres Row-Level Security.

Operational data

API request logs (path, status, latency, tenant_id, masked auth context), audit logs (immutable append-only), error backtraces (no PII).

3. What we DON'T collect

  • No advertising / tracking pixels.
  • No third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, no Hotjar, no segment.io).
  • No social-media trackers.
  • No cross-site cookies.

4. Cookies

Only first-party functional cookies are used:

  • weissman_session — JWT for your login session.
  • weissman_lang — your UI language preference (en / he).
  • weissman_cookie_consent_v1 — records that you saw the cookie banner.

No tracking cookies, ever.

5. Purpose & legal basis (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Contract (6(1)(b)) — running the Service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interest (6(1)(f)) — fraud prevention, abuse detection, securing the platform.
  • Legal obligation (6(1)(c)) — tax records, lawful disclosure requests.
  • Consent (6(1)(a)) — marketing emails (opt-in, opt-out anytime).

6. Sub-processors

Current production sub-processors:

  • AWS (eu-west-1) — primary infrastructure, encrypted at rest (KMS).
  • Paddle Inc. — payment processing (PCI DSS Level 1).
  • Cloudflare — DDoS protection, edge TLS.
  • SES / SendGrid — transactional email (no bulk marketing).

Full sub-processor list (with addition notification) at /subprocessors.html.

7. Data residency

Cloud SaaS: data is stored in EU-West (Ireland) by default. Enterprise customers may select US-East or AU-East. Self-hosted: data never leaves your infrastructure.

8. Retention

  • Account data: lifetime of account + 90 days after deletion.
  • Scan findings: 12 months by default, configurable per tenant.
  • Audit logs: 24 months (immutable).
  • Backups: 30 days rolling, encrypted.

9. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / LGPD)

You may request access, rectification, deletion ("right to be forgotten"), portability (JSON export), restriction of processing, or objection. Send requests to dpo@weissman.io — we respond within 30 calendar days. You may also lodge complaints with your supervisory authority (e.g. Israel Privacy Protection Authority, your country's Data Protection Authority).

10. Security

Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+), encryption at rest (AES-256 / KMS), TOTP-based MFA enforcement, signed audit log, RLS-per-tenant data isolation, mandatory pull-request review, ephemeral-sandbox patch verification, automated dependency scanning. Independent penetration test: annual.

11. Breach notification

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting your data, we will notify you and the relevant authority within 72 hours, per GDPR Art. 33.

12. Children

Service is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

13. Changes

Material changes will be announced 30 days in advance via email and dashboard banner.

14. Contact

DPO: dpo@weissman.io · Security: security@weissman.io
