Responsible Disclosure Policy

Effective: 2026-06-03 · We run a security platform — we know how this works.

If you find a vulnerability

Please email security@weissman.io with:

  • A clear description of the issue and the affected component.
  • Steps to reproduce (URLs, payloads, expected vs. observed).
  • Your name or handle for credit (optional — we honour anonymous reports too).

PGP: fingerprint 0A1B 2C3D 4E5F 6789 ABCD EF01 2345 6789 ABCD EF01 — public key at /.well-known/pgp-key.txt.

Our promise

  • Acknowledge receipt within 24 hours.
  • Triage and provide a severity assessment within 5 business days.
  • Patch critical issues within 14 days (high: 30 days, medium: 60 days).
  • Public credit in our changelog (with your consent).
  • No legal action for good-faith research that follows this policy.

Scope

In scope: *.weissman.io, the Cloud SaaS at app.weissman.io, our official binaries and Docker images, our open-source repositories.

Out of scope: third-party SaaS we use (report directly to them), social-engineering of staff, physical attacks, DoS attempts, automated scanner output without manual validation.

What we ask

  • Do not exfiltrate data beyond what's required to prove the issue.
  • Do not run automated scans that degrade service availability.
  • Give us a reasonable window to remediate before public disclosure (90 days is our default).

This program runs on goodwill — there is no monetary bounty today, but we celebrate every report publicly.
